- 期待与新的团队，一起创造新的品牌与产品。: AlimamaShuHeiTi-Bold.

Alimama ShuHeiTi is the unchanged official WOFF2 from https://fonts.alibabadesign.com/AlimamaShuHeiTi/AlimamaShuHeiTi-Bold/AlimamaShuHeiTi-Bold.woff2 (official site: https://fonts.alibabagroup.com/).
Alibaba PuHuiTi Regular/Medium are character subsets of the author's installed fonts; original names/notices preserved. Official publisher: https://fonts.alibabagroup.com/.
Bebas Neue is a Latin subset; OFL license included.

Previously tested IBM/Noto assets remain for rollback but are no longer referenced by typography.css.
